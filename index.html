How MUKRS Works

  • Each tournament result (0–1000) is duplicated by the number of tournament days
  • Minimum participation: 10 days of play (fewer = padded with 0s)
  • Part A (Consistency): Average of the top 80% of results (bottom 20% rounded down are dropped)
  • Part B (Achievements): Average of your best 8 results
  • Final MUKRS: Average of Part A and Part B
  • Only open MERS-rated events count; no weightings or decay within the selection period

Score & Position

Your MERS base rank at a tournament is calculated from your finishing position:

Base Rank = (Players − Position) / (Players − 1) × 1000

For example: 25th out of 100 players = (100−25)/(100−1)×1000 = 757
